Landlord field guide

Six mistakes I’ve made, so you don’t have to.

“You have a property you’re looking to rent out—congrats! Passive income is a powerful wealth builder. But there are a few common mistakes I’ve made myself and want to help you avoid.”

  1. Under- or over-pricing your rental

    Without a system or good data, your cash flow is at risk. Overpriced homes sit vacant; underpriced homes leave money on the table.

  2. Living far from your rental

    Prep work and showings take real time on-site. Living an hour or more away means long days, late nights, or missed opportunities.

  3. Underestimating the communication

    Every tenant call and message comes to you—plus the back-and-forth with contractors and vendors.

  4. Misunderstanding NC landlord–tenant law

    Ignorance of the law is no excuse for not following it.

  5. Leaving rent the same year after year

    Market rents change and need to be re-evaluated. And when people feel they’re underpaying for something, they tend to undervalue it.

  6. Having no systems in place

    Accounting, screening, marketing, and reliable contractors take time, experience, and trusted systems. Without them, mistakes cost revenue.
