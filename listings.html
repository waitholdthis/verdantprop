Renter FAQ

Good questions.

Still wondering? Call (910) 922-6519 or send us a note.

When can I schedule a showing?

Contact us or use the scheduler on any listing. Let us know the dates and times that work best for you and we’ll do our best to accommodate your schedule.

Who needs to fill out an application?

Any adult over the age of 18 will fill out an application and pay the non-refundable application fee.

Do you run background checks?

Yes. As part of your rental application, we request background checks from TransUnion that include criminal, eviction, and credit history. If you have concerns, discuss them with us before paying for the application.

Do you require a security deposit?

Yes. The amount varies per rental and can be anywhere from one to two months’ rent.

How long is the lease term?

Leases normally run for one year, sometimes with an option to renew. Depending on the property, we may be able to work with you on different terms.

How quickly can I move in?

We’ll work to get you moved in on the timeline that works best for you. Your move-in date depends on the status of the property you’re leasing.

What does the tenant portal do?

The Tenant Portal is an online tool provided at no cost. Pay rent online, view payments, add renter’s insurance, and submit or track maintenance requests.

How often is rent due and how can I pay?

Rent is due monthly, usually on the 1st. We recommend automatic online payment by credit card, debit card, or ACH.
